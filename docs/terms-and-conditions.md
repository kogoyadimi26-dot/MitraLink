## 1. Definitions

- **Platform:** the MitraLink marketplace, used through the web form, WhatsApp and the spreadsheet during the test, and the MitraLink app when launched.
- **Buyer:** a kios, warung, school, church or other business or organisation that registers to order goods.
- **Distributor:** a business that holds stock, accepts orders and ships goods to buyers.
- **Agent:** an individual who recruits buyers and relays orders for MitraLink under a separate agent agreement.
- **Order:** a request from a Buyer to a Distributor, identified by an order code (for example PDC-W-0001).
- **Confirmed Order:** an Order that the Buyer has marked as received (RECEIVED), after the Distributor has been paid and has shipped.
- **Commission:** the amount a Distributor owes MitraLink for each Confirmed Order. It is the higher of the category rate applied to the Order value and the minimum of Rp25,000 per Order.

## 2. What MitraLink does and does not do

2.1 MitraLink connects Buyers and Distributors, provides ordering and tracking, and invoices Commission.

2.2 During the test, MitraLink:
- does not sell or own goods;
- does not hold stock or ship goods;
- does not receive or hold Buyer payments. Buyers pay Distributors directly by bank transfer.

2.3 MitraLink is not a party to the sale between a Buyer and a Distributor.

## 3. Registration

## 4. Orders and payment

4.1 A Buyer places an Order through an Agent, the web form or WhatsApp. The Distributor accepts or declines it.

4.2 After acceptance, the Buyer pays the Distributor's bank account, quoting the order code in the transfer reference.

4.3 The Distributor must ship only after payment is confirmed from its bank statement, and must reply SHIP with the date.

4.4 The Buyer must confirm receipt (RECEIVED) within [number] days of delivery. If the Buyer does not respond, the Order is treated as confirmed after [number] days unless a dispute has been raised.

4.5 An Order counts toward the test targets only once it is a Confirmed Order.

5.2 Prices must include all amounts the Buyer has to pay, except where stated. The Distributor is responsible for applicable taxes on its sales.

5.3 The following goods are not allowed on the Platform: [list of restricted goods, for example alcohol, weapons, controlled medicines, counterfeit goods].

## 6. Commission

6.1 Distributors pay Commission for each Confirmed Order at the rate for its category:

| Category | Rate |
|---|---|
| Staples | 1.5% |
| School and office supplies | 3% |
| Household goods | 3% |
| Electronics and tools | 2% |

These rates are placeholders for the test and may change with [notice period] days' written notice.

6.2 The minimum Commission is Rp25,000 per Confirmed Order.

6.3 MitraLink invoices Commission weekly. Payment is due by bank transfer within [7] days of the invoice date.

6.4 If a Distributor does not pay within the due date, MitraLink may stop accepting new Orders from that Distributor until the invoice is paid.

6.5 A Distributor must not avoid Commission on an Order placed through the Platform, including by moving repeat orders off the Platform, for [period] after the Buyer's first Order.

## 7. Agents

7.1 Agents are independent. They have no authority to agree prices, accept payment, or bind MitraLink or a Distributor.

7.2 Agents are paid [Rp5,000, proposed] for each Confirmed Order, monthly, after the related Commission has been received.

7.3 Agents must not create fake Orders or Buyers. Payment is only due for Confirmed Orders.

7.4 Each Agent signs a separate agent agreement covering payment, tax treatment and conduct.

## 8. Disputes and returns

8.1 A Buyer who has a problem with an order (for example missing, damaged or wrong goods) must tell the Agent or MitraLink within [number] days of delivery, with the order code and evidence.

8.2 MitraLink will contact the Distributor and try to resolve the problem within [number] working days.

8.3 Refunds, replacements and returns are decided between the Buyer and the Distributor. MitraLink may record the outcome and take it into account for Distributor ratings or suspension.

8.4 Bank transfers are the evidence of payment. A screenshot alone is not proof of payment.

MitraLink may suspend or remove any account that breaks these rules.
