# Business plan: Papua Distribution Connect

## 1. Concept

A marketplace where kios, warung, schools and churches in Papua find and order from local distributors, and the distributor ships. We earn a commission from the distributor on each sale. We do not hold stock, ship goods, or touch the buyer's money during the test.

## 2. Problem

**Buyers can't find reliable suppliers.** Many kios depend on one or two distributors, or on trips to the city. They don't know who else sells what, or at what price.

**Distributors can't reach small buyers.** Most sales go to the few buyers they already know. Reaching villages and small shops is expensive for them.

**Connectivity is patchy.** Buyers need simple ordering that works through WhatsApp and local agents, not an app that needs a steady signal.

Supporting facts (as cited in the draft; not re-checked):

- 1,429 villages with no signal in Papua Pegunungan, the most in Indonesia ([GoodStats](https://goodstats.id/article/tak-semua-desa-punya-sinyal-internet-ini-sebarannya-di-indonesia-w8Xn6)).
- 56% of villages in Papua Pegunungan have no signal. Figure from the draft; source to confirm.
- Poverty: Papua Tengah 30.41%, Papua Pegunungan 26.34% ([Goodstats](https://data.goodstats.id/statistic/10-provinsi-dengan-persentase-penduduk-miskin-tertinggi-pada-maret-2026-C4ybx)).
- Small shops are growing: kelontong stores in one Jayapura study rose from 698 (2020) to 1,511 (2024) ([Journal](https://journal.aritekin.or.id/index.php/Konstruksi/article/download/1610/1661/9801)).

## 3. Solution

1. **Register:** buyers sign up free, through an agent or the web form.
2. **Browse:** distributor catalogues, filtered by town and product.
3. **Order:** the order goes straight to the distributor.
4. **Ship:** the distributor holds the stock and ships it.
5. **Confirm:** the buyer confirms what arrived. Disputes go to a set process.
6. **Invoice and pay:** the commission is calculated and invoiced to the distributor, who pays by bank transfer.

**What we never do:** hold stock, ship goods, or handle buyer payments (during the test).

## 4. Customers

- **Primary buyers:** kios and warung in Wamena and Jayapura.
- **Secondary buyers:** schools and churches buying in bulk.
- **Sellers:** distributors and larger toko with stock in those towns.

## 5. Why now

- **QRIS rule:** the plan cites a rule making QRIS free for small merchants from 1 October 2026 ([Antara](https://papua.antaranews.com/berita/767091/kki-dan-mdr-qris-0-persen-buka-luas-akses-ekonomi-digital-tanah-papua)). That date has passed, so confirm the rule is in force before citing it.
- **Shipping already reaches Papua.** The gap is connecting distributors to small buyers, not moving goods ([Tirto](https://tirto.id/jt-cargo-buka-rute-layanan-ke-papua-bukan-wilayah-konflik-gEv5)).
- **No capital needed** for stock or vehicles.

## 6. Operations

- **No stock, no fleet.** Distributors hold stock and ship.
- **Agents** in each town recruit buyers and help with orders by phone or WhatsApp. Agents are paid per confirmed order (see the business model).
- **Team:** one founder, plus agents.
- **Tools:** during the test, a spreadsheet and WhatsApp. Later, the MitraLink web app and payment gateway.

## 9. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Off-platform orders: buyers and distributors deal directly after the first order | Charge on repeat orders too. Provide value through ordering, invoicing and records. |
| Distributors don't pay | Invoice promptly. Block new orders against unpaid invoices. Sign written agreements. |
| Too few distributors | Sign distributors before recruiting buyers. |
| Shipping failures | Buyer confirmation, dispute process, distributor ratings. |
| Minimum fee pushback (Rp25,000 is 25% of a Rp100,000 order) | Test in week 1 with distributors. Adjust at the week 4 review. |
| Tax and licensing | Confirm obligations with a tax adviser before the first invoice. |
| Payment fraud (fake proof of transfer) | Confirm payment from the bank statement, not screenshots. Ship only after payment. |

## 10. Funding and next steps

- **Capital for the test:** near zero. Costs are a phone, WhatsApp and a spreadsheet.
- **Capital to build the app:** modest, mainly development time, funded by the founder or from own savings. Estimated CapEx is in the business model.
- **Next steps:**
  1. Finalise the distributor agreement.
  2. Finalise the catalogue sheet.
  3. Finalise the buyer form.
  4. Start the four-week test.
